social exclusion:141-143
-The concept of social exclusion refers to structural processes that prevent
particular people and groups from participating in the economic,
social, cultural, and political activities that other people in that society
access thereby resulting in a poor quality of life
-This definition highlights four aspects of social exclusion: social exclusion
is multidimensional(not able to acheive a socially acceptable standard of living: social institutions(family, education, welfare), full-time labour, labour contributing skills, social networks), leads to poverty and other negative outcomes (homelessness, fear of crime, mental illness, unemployment),
is linked to structural processes that are outside peoples’ control(not being in full time employment leaves youths feeling excluded, and that that exclusion is of their own doing, family dissolution(often from family violence, abandonment, divorce, or some other negative life
event resulting in low self esteem, depression, lack of integration in
social networks, work relations, and conventional society are all associated
with social exclusion, alongside worse school life. These people also have less resources to help them), and
arises among marginalized groups and areas(ethnicity, disability, drug abuse, alcoholism, homelessness,
criminal involvement,).
Summary:those who are shut out from relationships with family and communities,
come from disadvantaged backgrounds, experience unemployment,
poverty, and other negative outcomes, reside in underprivileged
communities, and are members of marginalized groups
